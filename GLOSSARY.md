# Outseta for Raycast

Look up people and accounts, and triage support cases in an Outseta account from Raycast.

## Support

**Case**:
A support conversation opened by a customer, with its replies, notes and status changes.
_Avoid_: Ticket

**Agent**:
A member of the Outseta account's team who handles Cases. Not a person in the CRM.
_Avoid_: User, admin

**Case filter**:
The criteria that pick which Cases to list: a status, and optionally an assignee (a specific Agent, or nobody). "Assigned to Me" is a Case filter on the Agent the user identified as themselves.
_Avoid_: View, queue
